4. Scientific Questions & Empirical Protocols

Can Golden Oyster mushrooms grow in hot summer conditions?

Yes. Pleurotus citrinopileatus thrives in warmer ambient temperatures between 70°F and 80°F, making it the premier warm-weather gourmet species.
