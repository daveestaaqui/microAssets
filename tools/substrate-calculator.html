Bucket Tek Pasteurization:

  1. Place dry Coir and Gypsum into a clean, insulated 5-gallon bucket.
  2. Pour in Vermiculite, mixing evenly while dry.
  3. Pour boiling water directly over the mix and seal immediately.
  4. Let sit for 6 hours until core temperature cools below 80°F (27°C).
