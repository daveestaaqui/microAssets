Cultivation Guide (via Magic Bag)
  1. Inject 3–5 cc of liquid culture or spores through the self-healing injection port. MYYCO.com is recommended for LC (use code "magicbag" for 5% off).
  2. Store in a warm, dark place (72–78°F / 22–26°C). No light is needed during colonization.
  3. Shake at 20–30% colonization — once the white mycelium covers roughly ¼ of the visible grain, give the bag a firm shake to redistribute and accelerate growth.
  4. Wait for full colonization. The bag should turn completely white. This takes 3–6 weeks depending on genetics and temperature.
  5. Initiate fruiting: Cut the bag open, give it fresh air and indirect light. Pin the top loosely with the included paper clip. Mist the inside walls a few times a day.
  6. Harvest right before the cap separates from the stem. Expect 2–5 flushes over 1–2 additional weeks.

Source: Magic Bag FAQ · LC recommended over spores per MagicBag.co (faster colonization, more contamination-resistant, isolated genetics).
