# Instagram Marketing Strategy — SporlyWorks

Establish @sporlyworks as the premier visual and scientific resource for home laboratory cultivation and clinical-grade adaptogenic wellness.

---

## 1. Content Pillars

We structure our content into three distinct, high-value pillars to keep our feed cohesive, balanced, and authoritative.

### 🔬 Pillar A: Adaptogens & Clinical Science (Wellness)
* **Goal:** Educate our community on the biochemical mechanisms of functional mushrooms.
* **Topics:** Nerve Growth Factor (NGF) stimulation from Lion's Mane, GABA pathways and Reishi triterpenes, ATP oxygen-kinetics and Cordyceps, and prebiotic gut survivability (Seed DS-01).
* **Tone:** Clinical, precise, informative, yet accessible. Avoid hype; use references to peer-reviewed studies.

### 🧫 Pillar B: Lab Diaries & Cultivation Science (Cultivation)
* **Goal:** Provide practical, high-end guidance for home mycological study.
* **Topics:** Biological efficiency (BE), agar work, contamination identification (Trichoderma vs. Aspergillus), substrate hydration physics (field capacity), and grain colonization timelines.
* **Tone:** Educational, technical, laboratory-focused.

### 📦 Pillar C: Product & Partner Showcases (Referrals)
* **Goal:** Connect our audience with our verified premium partners.
* **Topics:** Walkthroughs of North Spore grow kits, MYYCO liquid culture syringes, Magic Bag substrates, and FreshCap supplement powders.
* **Tone:** Review-focused, highlighting quality parameters (e.g., USDA organic, fruiting body extraction, lab sterility).

---

## 2. Weekly Posting Schedule (3x / Week)

Consistency is vital for algorithmic reach. We target high-engagement hours (between **8:00 AM – 10:00 AM** and **6:00 PM – 8:00 PM** local time).

| Day | Pillar | Focus | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Pillar A (Science) | Clinical study highlight + biochem breakdown | Square Graphic + scientific caption |
| **Wednesday**| Pillar B (Lab) | Diagnostic tips, substrate chemistry, or grow kit guide | Carousel Graphic + process checklist |
| **Friday** | Pillar C (Partner) | High-fidelity product showcase or protocol breakdown | Square Graphic + referral call-to-action |

---

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual System

To stand out from generic "dropshipping" or meme accounts, SporlyWorks operates with a **highly sophisticated, aesthetic editorial style** matching our website:

* **Background:** Textured cream/parchment background (`#fcfaf6`) for an organic, clinical-apothecary feel.
* **Typography:** Serif headings with clean sans-serif body copy (**Plus Jakarta Sans**).
* **Borders:** Thin gold and deep forest green frames to tie posts together.
* **Imagery:** Minimalist, hand-sketched botanical style illustrations, avoiding bright artificial filters.
