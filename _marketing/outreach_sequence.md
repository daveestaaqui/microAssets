# Mycology & Wellness Blog Outreach Sequence

This 3-step drip campaign is designed for reaching out to mycology educators, urban agriculture publications, biohacking/wellness blogs, and home biology content creators. It utilizes the PAS (Problem, Agitation, Solution) framework to establish content collaborations, guest articles, and resource cross-promotions.
