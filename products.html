Gourmet Cultivation vs. Laboratory Microscopy

Our curated selection supports two distinct mycological paths:

1. Gourmet Grow Kits (North Spore): Pre-colonized blocks of organic substrate that bypass inoculating and spawn-run stages. They are 100% legal, sterile-sealed, and guaranteed to fruit out of the box.

2. Microscopy & Scientific Research (MYYCO & Magic Bag): Isolated liquid culture spore solutions and sterile substrate bags for taxonomists, microscopists, and lab-scale spawn run studies.

LEGAL COMPLIANCE & SAFETY NOTICE: Psilocybe liquid cultures and spore solutions are sold strictly for microscopy, taxonomy, and scientific laboratory research purposes. Cultivation of active species remains federally restricted. We do not provide cultivation instructions or support for active species.
